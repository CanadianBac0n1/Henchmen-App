Our Founder & Visionary

Dr. Obscuro founded the Department of Unapologetic Mischief in the shadows of bureaucracy and brilliance. Once dismissed as "just another eccentric genius with a penchant for lasers," Dr. Obscuro's vision was clear: a stylish world where chaos thrives under well-documented procedure and pristine monologues.

Our Mission Statement

To sow tasteful chaos, instigate mildly inconvenient mayhem, and push the boundaries of ethical ambiguity—all while looking sharp and filing the appropriate HR paperwork.

Our Corporate History

Originally formed in a secret underground facility (conveniently situated beneath a less secret underground karaoke bar), D.U.M. quickly rose to global notoriety thanks to its commitment to innovation, theatrical flair, and quarterly team-building hostage simulations.

Executive Leadership

Dr. Obscuro remains at the helm as CEO (Chief Executive Oppressor), supported by a decorated council of experts in disinformation, trapdoor maintenance, and emotionally manipulative villain monologues.
