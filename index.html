Welcome to D.U.M.

The Department of Unapologetic Mischief is the global leader in stylish subversion, tasteful mayhem, and moderately villainous consultancy services under the direction of Dr. Obscuro. We specialize in the unthinkable—and occasionally the inconvenient.

Our Mission

To build a better tomorrow by slightly ruining today. We balance chaos and charisma, and pride ourselves on innovation, ambiguity, well-dressed disruption, and flawless monologuing.

Core Values

  • Loyalty—until the inevitable betrayal
  • Secrecy—even from ourselves
  • Precision in pettiness
  • Compliance with non-disclosure agreements, mostly
  • World domination—but make it stylish
